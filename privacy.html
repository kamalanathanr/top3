Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data Kamalanathan Raman ("we") collects through the Top3 service, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

1. Data we collect

  • Account details — your name and email address, provided at sign-up.
  • Phone number — provided by you, and used only to deliver the digests you requested over WhatsApp.
  • Interests — the topics you enter and any profile context you choose to add, used to select and rank results.
  • Generated content — the digests we produce for you, kept so you can read them again in the web app.
  • Technical logs — timestamps, delivery status and error messages, kept for reliability and troubleshooting.

We do not collect payment details. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

2. How we use it

Your data is used solely to operate the service: to run searches for your stated interests, to generate digests, and to deliver them to you by WhatsApp or in the web app. We do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

3. WhatsApp messaging

We send messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform, operated by Meta. We message only phone numbers that were entered by their owner during sign-up and explicitly opted in to delivery. We send account-related digests only — never marketing, and never on behalf of a third party.

You can stop messages at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by turning off WhatsApp delivery in your account settings. Delivery of your message content is subject to Meta's own privacy policy.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We share it only with:

  • Meta Platforms — your phone number and message text, as required to deliver WhatsApp messages.
  • Our hosting and infrastructure providers — who store data on our behalf under contract.
  • Authorities — where we are legally required to do so.

5. Retention

We keep account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we must retain records to meet a legal obligation. Technical logs are kept for 90 days.

6. Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it entirely. Write to kamalanathan.r@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is stored on access-controlled infrastructure and transmitted over encrypted connections. Access is limited to people who need it to operate the service.

8. Children

Top3 is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify registered users by email.
