Top3

A personal AI discovery service, delivered on WhatsApp

Top3 tracks the topics a person tells us they care about, searches for what is new and relevant, and sends them a short summary on WhatsApp. It is operated by Kamalanathan Raman, a sole proprietor based in Bengaluru, India.

What we do

A user signs in and lists the subjects they want to follow — for example "AI meet-ups in Bengaluru", "new releases in Kannada cinema", or "changes to Indian F&O margin rules". For each interest, Top3 runs a scheduled search across public web sources and video platforms, ranks the results, and produces a short digest of the most relevant items.

The digest is delivered to the phone number the user registered, over the WhatsApp Business Platform. Each message contains the item titles, a one-line summary and a link to the source.

How WhatsApp is used

Who receives messages
Only registered users who entered their own phone number and explicitly opted in to WhatsApp delivery during sign-up.
What is sent
Scheduled interest digests, and replies to questions a user sends us about their own account. Nothing else.
How often
At the frequency the user chooses — daily or weekly. Users can change or pause this at any time from their profile page.
How to stop
Replying STOP to any message ends WhatsApp delivery immediately. The same control is available in the account settings page.
What we never do
We do not send marketing or promotional messages, we do not message people who have not registered themselves, and we do not sell, rent or share phone numbers with third parties.

Who it is for

Individual users in India who want to follow a handful of niche topics without monitoring feeds themselves. Top3 is used directly by the people who sign up; we do not send messages on behalf of other businesses and we are not a messaging reseller or marketing platform.

How the service works

  • The user adds an interest in plain language on the Top3 web app.
  • Top3 matches the interest to a search strategy and queues a background job.
  • The job gathers results from public web and video sources and summarises them.
  • The summary is stored against the user's account and shown in the web app.
  • If the user opted in to WhatsApp, the same summary is sent to their number.

Data we hold

A user's account record contains their name, email address, phone number, the interests they entered, and the digests we generated for them. Message content is limited to those digests. Full detail, including retention periods and how to request deletion, is in our Privacy Policy.
